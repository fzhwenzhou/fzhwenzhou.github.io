I am a master’s student in Computer Science at Columbia University. My research interests span computer architecture, operating systems, compilers, and high-performance computing.

I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in June 2026. My earlier work includes RISC-V cryptographic acceleration and parallel computing, alongside experience as an undergraduate teaching fellow and HPC team captain.
