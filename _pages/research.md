## Current direction

My current research direction is **AI agents for computer architecture and operating systems**. My broader research interests are **computer architecture, operating systems, compilers, and high-performance computing**.

## Selected projects

### RISC-V cryptographic acceleration

*CUHK-Shenzhen · September 2023 – June 2025*

Designed a RISC-V instruction set extension for elliptic curve cryptographic acceleration. I built a general-purpose hardware accelerator template in Verilog suitable for multiple curves, and decomposed encryption and decryption into primitive operations to simplify instructions and reduce FPGA logic usage.

### GauU-Scene V2

*CUHK-Shenzhen · May – June 2024*

Parallelized CPU inference with Python multiprocessing and implemented multi-GPU training and inference in PyTorch, achieving speedups of up to **6.5×**. I also built a GitHub Pages website to visualize the dataset’s models and processing results.

## Research background

During my undergraduate studies at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, I worked on embedded systems and hardware–software co-design under Professor Yeh-Ching Chung. I also served as captain of the university’s high-performance computing team.

My technical experience includes **OpenMP, MPI, CUDA, OpenACC, Triton, and Slurm**, as well as **LLVM, GCC, Lex, and Yacc**. See my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}) for education, teaching, and industry experience.
